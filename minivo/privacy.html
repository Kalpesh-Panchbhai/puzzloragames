🎮 MINIVO

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 10, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 10, 2026

Minivo ("we", "our", or "us") is a casual mini-game collection built for fun. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains clearly what data we collect (very little) and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Minivo stores all game data locally on your device only. We do not have servers, accounts, or cloud sync. Specifically:

  • Game scores & preferences — stored in your device's local storage (Flutter SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device.
  • Favorites & recently played — stored locally to personalise your home screen.
  • No personal information — we never ask for your name, email, phone number, or any personally identifiable information.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage telemetry.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or show ads.
  • We do not use third-party tracking or analytics services.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

3. Third-Party Services

Minivo does not integrate any third-party SDKs that collect data. The app may link to external websites (such as this privacy policy page) — those sites have their own privacy policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Minivo is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13, and since we collect no personal information at all, there is nothing to safeguard beyond the device itself.

5. Data Security

Because all data lives locally on your device, your game data is protected by your device's own security mechanisms (passcode, Face ID, etc.). Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data permanently.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

7. Contact Us

Questions? Reach out at puzzloragames@gmail.com or visit puzzloragames.com.

🔒 Short version: Minivo stores your game scores locally on your device only. We collect no personal data, use no ads, and have no servers. Your data stays with you.
